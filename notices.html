Rookline Drive is a research and development project by ConvexRook. This case study includes a supplied LMU HUD reference, an early layered reconstruction, and captures of the current application renderer.

References and captures

The original HUD reference is retained in the October 7 reconstruction study. It is presented as reference material for explaining the development. Instrument examples use synthetic demonstration data. The desktop cockpit view is a game capture with its player name replaced for privacy. The source images represent different states; they do not establish exact visual or behavioral parity.

The Interlagos animation is a development preview based on recorded weather, with illustrative local drying rates. Its local timing demonstrates the intended spatial fade.

Flag motion

The displayed flag composition uses motion adapted from Google Noto Animated Emoji, CC BY 4.0. Checkerboard removed and custom artwork support added. Drive provides a two-second continuous wave and a four-second full sequence. No Google endorsement is implied.
