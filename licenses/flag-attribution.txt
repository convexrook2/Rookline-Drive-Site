Modifications: removed 32 checkerboard vector/matte layers; retained the
original cloth keyframes, shape geometry, timing, occlusion, and pole.
The pole-free Lottie hides the pole drawing while preserving its parenting
transforms. The reusable JavaScript renderer samples the original vector
geometry at 60 frames per second and adds reconstructed image deformation.
Drive interpolates compatible adjacent sampled poses on its existing HUD frame
clock. The continuous-wave excerpt repeats in two seconds; the full sequence,
including its ending, repeats in four seconds. The supplied source poses remain unchanged.
The exported animated HTML retains a source and license attribution comment.
